About Harpalyke

Designation:
Also known as Jupiter XXII and formerly designated S/2000 J 5
Type:
A small retrograde irregular moon of Jupiter with a mean radius of about 2.2 kilometers.
Orbit:
The moon orbits Jupiter at a mean distance of 21.1 million kilometers and completes one orbit in 624.5 Earth days.
Discovered:
Found on Nov. 23, 2000 by Scott S. Sheppard, David C. Jewitt, Yanga R. Fernandez, and Eugene Magnier at the Mauna Kea Observatory in Hawaii.
Name Origin:
Named after Harpalyke, a figure in Greek mythology who was the daughter of Klymenos.
